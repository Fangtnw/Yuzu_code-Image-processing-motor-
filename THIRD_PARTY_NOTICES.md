# Licensing and release contact

The top-level MIT text documents the license already declared by the
application package; it does not relicense third-party material. The project
owner must confirm ownership and distribution approval before external release.

- ICube EtherCAT driver: Apache-2.0. Imported from the pinned upstream revision;
  keep upstream copyright headers, LICENSE and any notices. The driver extension
  in `patches/` modifies that component and retains its licensing context.
- Oriental Motor and MISUMI PDFs and XML: manufacturer reference material,
  not covered by the application MIT license. Source links are in `vendor/`.
  Confirm redistribution terms before sending copies externally; retain source
  links if copies cannot be distributed.
- ROS 2, IgH EtherCAT and system packages retain their respective licenses.

The package contact `maintainer@example.invalid` is an intentional non-deliverable
placeholder, not a support address. Replace it with the organization's approved
contact in both package.xml and setup.py before a customer release.
