# Oriental Motor reference links

Manufacturer PDFs and ESI XML are excluded from this operator bundle. The
integrated runtime uses its supplied YAML mappings. For installation and
engineering reference, obtain the applicable documents from the manufacturer:

- [AZDxA-KED rev0301 ESI archive](https://www.orientalmotor.com/support/software/SFTWR/ORIENTALMOTOR_AZDxA-KED_rev0301.zip)
- [AZ multi-axis EtherCAT manual HM-60323-7E](https://www.orientalmotor.com/products/pdfs/opmanuals/HM-60323-7E.pdf)
- [AZ family catalog](https://www.orientalmotor.com/products/pdfs/2018-2019/502%20AZ%20Family%20Catalog.pdf)

The supplied AZD3A-KED identity is vendor `0x000002BE`, product `0x000013AF`,
revision `0x01110301`; confirm the installed device before selecting its ESI.

See [Motor speed sources](MOTOR_SPEED_SOURCES.md) for model-specific sources,
configured operating caps and software acceleration profiles. Manufacturer
materials retain their respective copyrights and terms.
